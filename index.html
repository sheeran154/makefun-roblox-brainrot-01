Meme Menagerie

Brainrot Bazaar — collect them, stack them, steal them.

A 3D Roblox brainrot game: absurd meme-born characters parade down the bazaar conveyor, each earning money every second. Buy them, park them on your base, climb rarities and mutations, rebirth for permanent bonuses — and sprint home when the tsunami wave sweeps the map.

How it plays

  • Collect — Sir Quacksalot, Professor Pickle, Banana Bandit, DJ Toaster, Grandma Turbo and more.
  • Earn — every brainrot pays $/s. Rarities run Common to Secret; mutations pay Golden x2, Diamond x5, Rainbow x10.
  • Steal — carry a rival's brainrot home, but watch for the Slap Glove.
  • Survive — every 3 minutes a tsunami sweeps the bazaar. Outside your base, you get flung and lose 10% of your cash.
  • Rebirth — cash it all in for +25% income forever, and climb again faster.
